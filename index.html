Why Bastion is on Zin and not Kloe

The Japanese guide this build started from puts Bastion in Kloe's white slot in place of EP 3. Checked against the Arts table that matches the game:

The guide says Bastion is what enables Earth Wall on her line. It is not: Earth Wall needs Earth 4 / Space 3 and Mercy already supplies the Earth. This is a calculation, not a guide's recommendation. To follow the guide instead, put Bastion in Kloe's lower-left slot and EP 4 in Schera's bottom slot.
