The fights in Mistwald inflict Sleep and Confusion. The default accessories only protect Kloe from Confusion.

  • Upgrade costs: any Sphere+ is 3 U-Material, Lily Necklace+ is 2, Black Bangle X is 1.
  • Spheres are sold at Elger's in Rolent. Check his stock for Black Bangle and Lily Necklace; guides disagree on whether he carries them.
  • One enemy there takes only physical damage and another takes only Arts.
  • When the boss makes copies, hit only the one that casts a shadow. Hitting a copy damages your party.
  • Put the default accessories back afterwards.

Battle plan

Boss opener

  1. Estelle → Morale EX
  2. Zin → self STR/DEF buff
  3. Kloe → Kaempfer β if its debuff lands, otherwise Chaos Brand or a weakness Art
  4. Schera → Heaven's Kiss when it catches most of the party

After that

  • Schera Heaven's Kiss › Orbal Down when the boss is casting › Aero Storm / weakness Arts
  • Kloe emergency heal › debuff › weakness Arts. Save her S-Break for emergencies.
  • Zin self-buff › Taunt as needed › physical Crafts
  • Estelle keep Morale up › Earth Guard on Zin › interrupt › damage

Whether a given boss resists Orbal Down or Chaos Brand is untested.

Notes

Why Bastion is on Zin and not Kloe

The Japanese guide this build started from puts Bastion in Kloe's white slot in place of EP 3. Checked against the Arts table that matches the game:

EP 4 in the slot (this build)Bastion in the slot (guide)
Max EP+40%none
Tearal, Tear-Allyeslost
Lost Mobius, White Gehenna, Silver Thorn, Sylphen Wing, Chaos Brandyeslost
Atlas Hammer, Titanic Roarnoyes
Shields on Kloe ×3noyes

The guide says Bastion is what enables Earth Wall on her line. It is not: Earth Wall needs Earth 4 / Space 3 and Mercy already supplies the Earth. This is a calculation, not a guide's recommendation. To follow the guide instead, put Bastion in Kloe's lower-left slot and EP 4 in Schera's bottom slot.
