              How gear is picked
              • Three lines per chapter. Shops sell one weapon per character, one armour and one footwear. Chests hold a second line: armour and footwear with STR or with ATS on top, and for some characters a stronger weapon. There is one of each.
              • Upgrades. An orbal factory turns a piece into its + version for U-Material and monster parts. A chest piece is a little behind the upgraded shop piece until it is upgraded too, and ahead of it afterwards.
              • Ranking. Each piece is scored for the character: their attack stat (STR or ATS) and both defences count in full, the other attack stat a fifth, a point of SPD as eight. The best piece they can have right now is the one shown.
              • Men's and women's cuts. Chest armour and footwear can only be worn by men or only by women. Shop gear and weapons have no such limit. A piece is never suggested for someone who cannot wear it.
              • One copy. A chest piece goes to whoever gets the most out of it. On a tie the chapter's priority order decides.
              • Materials. Upgrades are paid for from your bag in order of gain per U-Material. What you cannot afford yet is listed as waiting, not as a step.
              • Reserve. Characters on the bench only get what is spare. Nothing is bought or upgraded for them.
              How orbments work
              • Lines. Every slot sits on a line that starts at the center. The center slot counts for every line.
              • Elemental values. Each quartz adds points to one or more of the seven elements. The game adds them up per line; that is the Value box in the orbment menu.
              • Arts. An Art unlocks when a single line reaches its requirement. Points on different lines do not combine, which is why a character with one long line casts far more than one with four short lines.
              • Locked slots. A slot with a coloured arc above it only takes quartz of that element. White slots take anything.
              • One of a kind. Two quartz of the same family, such as Action 3 and Action 4, cannot sit on one orbment.
              • Ranks. A higher rank of a quartz usually adds one elemental point and a little more of its effect. The extra point is often what unlocks the next Art.
              • Resistances. 100% is immunity. Lower numbers are a chance, and the best single source counts on this page.

              Known source errors, so they are not reintroduced:

              • Silver Intention's Arts table lists Time for the Tear line. The game uses Mirage.
              • Kago lists Jade Guard as Wind 2 and Topaz Guard as Earth 2. The game has Wind 3 and Earth 3.
              • Earlier versions listed “Divine Cross” and “Black Bangle X”. The English names are Divine Cloth and Black Bangle+.
              • Earlier versions said “Elize Turnpike”. The game calls it Elize Highway.
              • Earlier versions gave Breeze an item range of +30m. The game's table has +10m.
              • The guide's accessory list leaves out SPD +5 on Force Seal+ and Victory Headband. The game's table has it.

              Not verified: whether specific bosses resist specific debuffs, and what “All status ailments” covers exactly.
